Insulin Sensitivity Restoration (ISR™) Treatment

ISR Treatment is a cutting-edge therapy designed to restore insulin sensitivity, particularly for patients suffering from diabetic complications. Utilizing proven pulsatile insulin infusion technology, ISR therapy provides effective treatment for various diabetic conditions, including:

Our Treatment Focus
Diabetic Kidney Disease: Restoring insulin sensitivity to manage diabetic kidney complications
Uncontrolled Blood Sugar: Improving glucose metabolism and insulin efficiency.
Insulin Resistance: Addressing the root cause of insulin resistance to improve overall health.
Diabetic Complications: Offering a holistic treatment plan for managing the long-term effects of diabetes.

Our team of specialists ensures a personalized and effective ISR treatment plan, helping you regain control of your health.
