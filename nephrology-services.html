Nephrology Services

Our nephrology specialists offer expert care for kidney-related conditions, providing walk-in, walk-out consultations and treatments for:

Our Treatment Focus
Kidney Insufficiency/Failure: Comprehensive management options, including dialysis when necessary.
Chronic Kidney Disease (Stages 1-4): Early detection, monitoring, and treatment for all stages of kidney disease.
Kidney Stones: Diagnosis and treatment for kidney stones, with an emphasis on prevention.
Acute Kidney Injury: Immediate care for acute kidney injury and recovery support.
Diabetic Kidney Disease: Specialized treatment for kidney damage caused by diabetes.
Proteinuria, Hematuria, and Electrolyte Imbalances: Addressing abnormal levels of protein, blood in urine, and electrolyte disturbances.
Glomerulonephritis & Nephrectomy: Treatment for kidney inflammation and surgical procedures if needed.
