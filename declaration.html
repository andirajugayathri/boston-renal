Our Declaration

Boston Renal - Our Declaration

At Boston Renal, we believe in putting our patients first. Your health, trust, and experience matter deeply to us, and we're committed to delivering care with transparency, compassion, and ethics. Here's how we make that happen:

1. We Don't Play Favorites with Brands:
  • No commissions, gifts, or incentives from pharmaceutical companies, labs, or hospitals. Your health is our priority—not any brand's agenda. We stick to prescribing medications that are effective, affordable, and, whenever possible, generic. If we ever accept samples, it's only to help patients who truly need them.
2. Clear and Honest Billing:
  • No surprises, no hidden fees. You'll know the costs of your treatment upfront. If unexpected medical needs arise, we'll make sure to explain any additional charges clearly and promptly. Payments go directly to the right place—whether that's the pharmacy, diagnostic lab, or hospital. Rest assured, we don't profit from emergencies or critical situations.
3. Safety and Transparency with CCTV:
  • For your safety and ours, our facility uses CCTV to monitor consultations and procedures. This ensures accountability and helps us maintain the highest standards of care. All recordings are securely stored and used only when absolutely necessary for training or quality improvements.
4. Your Privacy Matters:
  • Your medical records, reports, and photographs are handled with the utmost care and confidentiality. We never share your information without your consent. If you allow us to use your case or images for educational purposes, we'll ask you to sign a consent form—and as a thank-you, you may receive a small discount on your treatment.
5. We're Here to Talk:
  • Have questions or concerns? We're all ears. Our team is always happy to explain your treatment options in detail. While we prioritize in-person consultations to ensure thorough care, we're working hard to make telehealth and virtual consultations more accessible within legal and medical guidelines.
6. Expert Care, Always:
  • Every procedure and treatment is handled exclusively by the dedicated Boston Renal team. We've partnered with some of the best facilities to ensure you receive the care you deserve, but it's always our experts overseeing your journey.
A Promise from Us to You:

At Boston Renal, we don't just treat kidneys—we care for people. Our mission is to make your healthcare experience as stress-free, transparent, and compassionate as possible. Your trust means everything to us, and we're honored to be a part of your journey to better health.
